Pricelist Items Manager

Easy Management of Pricelist Items in Odoo 18

Key Features

  • Dedicated menu for pricelist items under Sales > Products
  • Comprehensive list view with all relevant fields
  • Easy deletion of pricelist items
  • Proper access rights management for sales managers

Easy to Use

Simply navigate to Sales > Products > Pricelist Items to access a comprehensive list of all pricelist rules in your system. The interface allows you to easily sort, filter, and delete unwanted entries.

Benefits

Time Saving

Quickly locate and manage pricelist items without navigating through multiple menus and screens.

User-Friendly

Intuitive interface that makes pricelist management accessible to all users with proper permissions.

Maintenance

Keep your Odoo system clean by easily removing outdated or unnecessary pricelist rules.
